## Promotional text

Dictate, type, and control your Mac from iPhone while you walk. On-device voice processing, no Vibe Walkie account, and no subscription.

## Description

Vibe Walkie turns your iPhone into a private remote for your Mac—ideal when you want to code, write, or review work while walking.

Dictate text without sending your voice to the Internet, use a keyboard and trackpad, choose the right app or window, and optionally view your Mac screen.

The standard connection goes directly over your local Wi-Fi. Optional Remote Mode can use your own Tailscale network when you are away. No Vibe Walkie account is required. The free Mac companion installs from a signed and notarized DMG.

To get started:

1. download the Mac companion from `vibewalkie.app`;
2. open it and follow the guided Accessibility permission step;
3. scan its QR code with your iPhone;
4. approve the iPhone on your Mac;
5. send your first command.

Privacy: audio is transcribed on-device on your iPhone and is never sent to the Mac. Transcripts are not stored. There is no Vibe Walkie account, advertising, or third-party analytics.

Requires iOS 26, macOS 15 or later, and an Apple silicon Mac. Standard mode requires both devices on the same local network. Remote Mode requires Tailscale installed separately on both devices and access to the same tailnet; Tailscale has its own terms.
